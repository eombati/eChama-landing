Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Still have questions? Contact our support team.

Does my group pay anything to use eChama?
No. During launch there is no group subscription and no limit on members or groups. If we introduce group plans later, we will email you at least 30 days before any charge.
What does a member pay for an in-app payment?
A $1.50 eChama fee, plus amounts equal to Stripe's ACH processing fee (0.8% of the contribution, up to $5) and payout fee (0.25%). On a $100 contribution the member pays $102.55 and the group receives the full $100. The breakdown and total are shown before every payment.
Why are there processing and payout amounts?
Stripe charges eChama to debit the member's bank and to pay the money out to your group's bank. Those costs are passed through at the same rates so the group never loses part of a contribution to fees.
Is there a fee if a member pays outside the app?
No. Payments made outside the app — Zelle, check, cash or a bank transfer — carry no eChama fee. Admins can record them, and if your group connects its bank account, eChama can match deposits to members automatically.
Does Auto-Pay cost more?
No. Auto-Pay debits use the same fees as any in-app payment, and the confirmation email shows the contribution, the fees and the total debited.
What happens if a payment fails?
A failed payment is never recorded as a contribution, and you'll be told by email. Our Terms (§5.5) allow a returned-payment fee of up to $9.00 for payments returned for insufficient funds; we'll email at least 30 days before we start charging it.
Do members need to download anything or create an account?
Each member needs a free eChama account to accept an invitation, view their contribution history and make in-app payments. There's nothing to download — eChama runs in the browser on any device.
Is eChama available outside the United States?
eChama is currently available only for savings groups in the United States. In-app payments and bank monitoring require US bank accounts.
